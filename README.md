# Ethical Presence Anchoring Community Group

This is the working repository of the W3C
[Ethical Presence Anchoring Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/ethicalpresence/).

## Why this group exists

Technology is moving into the home. Speakers, screens, cameras, sensors
and spatial computing devices now sit in bedrooms, kitchens and living
rooms. Whoever controls those devices can learn how a family lives.

This group starts from one belief:

> **A home's digital space belongs to the people who live there.
> No company, platform or person should profit from it without them.**

## Principles (draft for discussion)

1. **The people come first, not the technology.** Spatial technology in
   the home should serve the people living in it: their safety, dignity,
   relationships and daily life.
2. **The home holds its own data.** What happens in a home should be able
   to stay in that home: stored there, processed there, and controlled
   there.
3. **Consent is explicit, specific and revocable.** Nothing is shared by
   default, and anyone can take back what they shared.
4. **The household decides who sees what.** Adults, children, guests and
   caregivers have different needs. Those boundaries belong to the
   household, not to a vendor.
5. **Protection for those who cannot protect themselves.** Children,
   elders and people receiving care deserve stronger defaults, not weaker
   ones.
6. **Oversight is visible.** When someone with authority looks at
   another person's information, that act should be accountable and never
   silent.
7. **No extraction.** A home is not a source of advertising, behavioral
   data or attention to be sold.
8. **Devices earn trust before they get it.** A device should prove what
   it is and what it does before it joins a home.

## What this group may produce

- A statement of ethical principles for spatial and presence technology
  in the home.
- Guidance and design patterns for local-first, consent-based home
  technology.
- Where useful, open specifications that let devices respect these
  principles while working together.

## Participating

Developers, designers, researchers, clinicians, caregivers, ethicists and
families are all welcome. Join the group on the
[W3C group page](https://www.w3.org/community/ethicalpresence/), then open
an issue to start a discussion.
